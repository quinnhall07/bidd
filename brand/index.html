The I is a 1.

Everywhere people read it, the name is Bidd. The logo draws it B1DD: the I becomes a 1, the serial number on a one-of-one piece. The letters always print in ink, black on white and white on black. The 1 belongs to the campus and takes its color. Before anyone signs in, the 1 is ink too.

Clear space

Keep the width of the 1 empty on every side of the logo.

Minimum size

Wordmark 64px wide. Below that, use the B1 mark, which holds down to the 16px favicon.

No lockups

Never join the Bidd logo to a university logo, chapter letters or a crest. In the demo, a school’s logo can identify its campus, always set apart from ours.

Never retype it

The logo is outlined from Archivo with its own spacing. Use the files; typing B1DD gets it wrong.

Never color the lettersThe B and D stay ink. Only the 1 takes a color.
No outside colorsThe 1 is a campus color or ink, nothing else.
Never stretch itScale the mark evenly, or not at all.
No effectsNo shadows, outlines, gradients or glow.

Bidd frames. Campus fills.

Bidd is black and white and nothing else. A campus color only ever arrives as a fill: the 1 in the logo, drop banners, Closet Cup bars, the active chip, focus rings. One row in campuses sets it, and the kit derives four values from it so it reads on any ground. A navy or maroon that would vanish next to black letters gets lifted with its hue kept, and a black campus hands the 1 to its second color.

Never set body copy in the condensed italic, and never set the italic in lowercase. Headlines stay short: two to four words, ending on a period when it's a statement.

Say it like a drop.

Bidd talks like someone in your chapter who knows fashion, not like a brand deck. Short, sure, specific.

  • Say less.

    Two to four words for a headline. Buttons are verbs: List it, Buy it, Pre-order.

  • Numbers win.

    "48 left" and "02:14:09" beat "almost gone" and "ending soon".

  • Talk campus.

    Rush, Bid Day, gameday, formal, the Cup. Use the words chapters already use.

  • No fake hype.

    Never invent scarcity, prices or partners. A drop is limited because the chapter set the stock.

Theme

Tokens for Tailwind v4 and shadcn/ui: black primary, campus ring and fills, the display and label utilities.

app/globals.css, after Tailwind and shadcn
@import "../brand/theme/bidd.css";

Fonts

Archivo for display and Hanken Grotesk for text, both through next/font.

app/layout.tsx
import { archivo, hanken } from "@/brand/theme/fonts";

<html lang="en" className={`${archivo.variable} ${hanken.variable}`}>

Campus colors

Turns a campuses row into the four fill variables. Put it on <body> once the campus is known.

app/layout.tsx
import { campusTheme } from "@/brand/theme/campus";

<body style={campusTheme({
  primary: campus.primary_color,
  secondary: campus.secondary_color,
})}>

Logo components

React SVGs. The letters follow currentColor, the 1 follows the campus.

any component
import { BiddWordmark, BiddMark } from "@/brand/react/bidd-logo";
